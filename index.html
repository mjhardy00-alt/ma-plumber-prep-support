MA Plumber Prep Support

MA Plumber Prep is a Massachusetts plumbing exam study and reference app with practice questions, exam practice, and an offline Massachusetts code library.

Contact / Support

Email: Mjhardy00@gmail.com

For help with a question or the app, open a practice question and tap Report Question. Choose a reason (or Other for general support), add a note, then tap Send Report. Your email app opens a draft for you to review and send.

Privacy

No account or sign-in is required. The app saves your answers, Saved questions, and practice position locally on your device. It does not automatically send that study progress or personal information to us.

If you choose to report a question, the app opens your email app with question details and any comment you entered. We receive that information only if you send the email. Links to external code sources open only when you choose them.

MA Plumber Prep is an independent study aid and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by the Commonwealth of Massachusetts or any examination provider.
